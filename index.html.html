About Us

Hereafter was founded with love — not just for the art of invitation design, but for every creative detail that turns big moments into unforgettable memories.

Our team brings together a shared passion for graphic design and a lifelong curiosity for the creative side of things.

We specialize in digital-only invitations, crafted for every occasion — from weddings and debuts to birthdays, corporate events, anniversaries, and more. Going beyond static designs, we bring each client's story to life through thoughtful, personalized details in every layout we create.

Here, flexibility is key: we listen, adapt, and always strive to deliver designs you'll truly love.

Ordering Process

ServiceOrdering InstructionsWhat You Can Expect
Invitation Design
  • Tell us your story, theme, colors, fonts, and any inspiration you have.
  • Provide names, date, venue, guest list, roles, dress code, RSVP info, etc.
  • We'll send 2–3 digital layouts within 1–2 days; request unlimited revisions.
  • Approve the final design — we'll deliver your ready-to-share digital files.
 Multiple design options, unlimited revisions, final files delivered promptly after approval.
Save the Date Photo Edits
  • Send us your chosen photos (up to 5 for the base rate).
  • Tell us what text to include and your preferred design style.
  • We'll send edited layouts for your approval.
  • Let us know if you'd like any adjustments.
  • Once approved, we'll send all digital files via Drive.
 Edited photo layouts, additional edits available on request, final files after approval.
Logo / Monogram Design
  • Share your initials/names, event or brand theme, and any style references you like.
  • Let us know where the logo will be used (invite, signage, packaging, etc.) so we design for it.
  • We'll send 2–3 initial concepts within 1–2 days.
  • Choose a direction and refine through up to 2 revision rounds.
 Final logo files in color + black & white, with a transparent-background version included.
Pub Mat
  • Send event details — what's being announced, date, and any key info to highlight.
  • Share your preferred colors, mood, or reference designs.
  • We'll send 1–2 layout options within 1 day.
  • Request one round of minor text or detail revisions.
 Feed-ready file delivered promptly; story-size version available on request.
RSVP Page
  • Send us your invitation design (if ready) so the page matches your theme.
  • Provide event details, program flow, and any sections you want included (map, gallery, countdown, etc.).
  • We'll share a preview link within 3–5 days for your review.
  • Request up to 3 rounds of revisions before we finalize.
  • Once approved, your page goes live and starts collecting RSVPs.
 A live, shareable RSVP link with guest responses organized for easy headcount tracking.

General Notes for All Products

  • All products are digital only — no physical printing.
  • Turnaround times vary by project complexity; we'll confirm your timeline after receiving your details.
  • Rush requests may be accommodated for an additional fee, depending on our current workload.
  • We require a 50% down payment before we start any design work.
  • Final files are released only once the remaining balance has been settled.
